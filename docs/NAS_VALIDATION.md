8. Cancel or interrupt a multi-item workflow. Capture succeeded, failed, unattempted and uncertain items. Confirm an uncertain operation is not automatically repeated. Request an inverse plan and approve it separately.
9. Test masked privacy findings on synthetic positives/negatives, encrypted documents, document-contained instructions and unavailable model/locality. Unknown or unreadable must not become safe.
10. Save an explicitly approved redacted report to a new destination and verify its real path/contents. An existing destination must block overwrite. Distinguish visible history from durable audit controls.
11. Measure the real metrics described in EVALUATION.md; retain raw observations and failures. Capture demo footage only after successful device execution. Export permitted test evidence before the assigned device reset.
